This illustrated reading edition brings together 22 stories from all three recordings, in interview order. It includes Rose’s childhood, the wider family, weddings, work, and her bond with Renee. The full unedited recordings and supplied transcripts are available in the downloads.

Filler sounds, accidental stutters, repeated acknowledgements, some abandoned fragments and recording-related asides are removed. Adjacent pieces of the same person’s speech are sometimes joined across these interruptions. Meaningful repetitions, questions, laughter, uncertainty and recollections are retained. The headings and story divisions are editorial.

This is a gently edited reading version, not a verbatim transcript. Orit’s opening explanation is spoken in Hebrew; its English text follows the translation in the supplied transcript. The recording passages have been matched to the pages using local speech recognition. The supplied third transcript interchanges Rose and Orit’s names in many places; clear speaker assignments are corrected in the reading text. The original transcript downloads preserve the supplied labels. This is not a full verification of the transcript’s wording or speaker labels; uncertain names, dates and expressions remain as transcribed.

Listening starts at the current page and continues through the remaining pages and stories automatically, stopping at the end of the book. Pause stops both the audio and automatic page turning. The recording includes the other speakers and speech clutter removed from the reading text. Within each selected passage, the recording preserves the original conversation, pauses, and replies. Between passages it skips recording interruptions and incidental digressions. The complete recordings remain uncut in the audio download.

A soft highlight follows the paragraph being spoken. Paragraph timings are estimated from locally recognized words; a few brief or overlapping replies have approximate timing. Pausing keeps your place highlighted.

The illustrations are imagined watercolors of places and objects. They contain no people.
